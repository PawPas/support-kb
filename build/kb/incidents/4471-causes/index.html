#4471 · two bugs, not one

owner Pawel Paskudzki resolved reviewed 2026-10-28 · 0 days ago

#4471 was merged on subject lines (decision 0001): every report whose subject sounded like a wrong date went into one ticket, “dates parse mm/dd in eu locales”. That counts the noise once, which is what the desk wanted. But the desk’s own cause logic (src/lib/kb/causes.ts) does not agree that it is one bug.

Run the cause check over the 412 reports and they fall into two groups with two different fixes. One ticket cannot track two fixes.

The two problems

parser — the shared parser reads every date month-first. A day-first (EU) tenant’s dates come out flipped: 7 April becomes 4 July. This is the bug the desk already knows, and it covers most of the ticket. Fix: parse each tenant’s dates in that tenant’s locale. Filed as ticket #1; fixed and closed completed (fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales).

hint — the booking-v3 form hints MM/DD/YYYY. Day-first users follow the hint and type the month first; the form then stores the value the tenant’s day-first way, so the date is already wrong at entry, before the parser runs. Only form reports from the three booking-v3 tenants (alder.physio, copper.studio, pale.harbour) land here. Fixing the parser does not fix these. Fix: make the form hint match the tenant’s locale. Filed as ticket #2; closed won’t fix — the booking-v3 form was retired, so the bad hint is gone with it.

Resolved (desk update, 5 Oct)

The desk closed #4471 on 5 October with two outcomes, one per cause — the proof that it was never one bug:

  • parser → FIXED: fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales.
  • hint → WON’T FIX: booking-v3 form retired.

Why one ticket hid two bugs

The merge rule reads the subject line, never the body or the tenant. Two bugs produce the same complaint — “my date is wrong” — so both flowed into #4471. Intake’s intakeTag does not rescue this: it guesses from the reporter’s browser, and it disagrees with the real cause on 38 reports (tagged hint but really parser, and the other way round).

Lessons

  • Merge on subject to count fast, but re-check the cause before you call a ticket “known”.
  • A number that can be computed belongs in a component: the counts above come from causes.ts, so they cannot drift from the data.
